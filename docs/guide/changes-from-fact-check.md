## kaikoura

## nelson-tasman

## west-coast

## dunedin-otago

### Why Come to Dunedin & the Otago Coast

- ~~Most~~ **Plenty of** visitors give Dunedin ~~a single~~ **just one** night on the way through, and that's ~~the usual~~ **a common** regret. The wildlife here keeps its own hours. ## southland-catlins
