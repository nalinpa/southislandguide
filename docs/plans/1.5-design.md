# 1.5 and the ad funnel — design

Owner's decisions, 2026-11-19 and 2026-11-20. Design only: the build plan comes after the owner approves this, and each task runs on the owner's go.

## Why

The owner wants to run Google Ads for installs. Two things are missing: the app reports nothing Google Ads can count, and kiwiguide.app is a single landing page with nothing that pulls an ad click towards the app. The numbers from both also feed the auto trip planner's design (1.6).

## What it is

Two parts, shipped separately:

1. **The quiz funnel on kiwiguide.app** (ships first, no app change): a Blinkist-style web quiz ("the type of funnel I want", owner, pointing at blinkist.com/onboarding): one question per screen with a progress bar, a reveal of the visitor's suggested trip, then **an account made on the site with that trip saved to it**, then the App Store. Signing in to the app with the same email shows the trip.
2. **1.5 store build:** Google Analytics for Firebase in the app (ten events) and a "Rate Kiwi Guide" row in Profile.

Ad flow: Google Ads (Search / Performance Max, web) → `kiwiguide.app/plan/` → questions → the reveal → account + trip saved (the Google Ads conversion) → App Store → install (Apple's campaign report) → sign in, the trip is there → `first_open` (Firebase, once 1.5 is live).

## Goals

- Google Ads bids on something real: an account with a saved trip; the App Store tap as a second signal; `first_open` once 1.5 is out.
- The visitor never picks their trip twice: it's in the app when they sign in.
- One Google Analytics property for the app (Firebase) and the website (a web stream), so the funnel reads in one place, with the drop-off at every step.
- The trip shown and saved is exactly one of the app's 26 suggested trips.

## Non-goals

- **No custom trips yet** (owner: "start without custom trips, just suggested trips"). Considered and kept for later: a generator, trips stitched from checked pieces, and the visitor building place by place with suggestions (owner's direction for the next step; TASKS.md, Parked).
- ~~No Apple or Google sign-in on the site~~ **Changed (owner, 2026-11-20):** Google sign-in on the site now (build Task 4b), Apple sign-in later in 1.5, after the App ID swap (Task 13). Both by redirect, with no third-party script. The same Google or Apple account signs in to the app and finds the trip.
- No web paywall, no trip codes, no editing the trip on the site.
- No api change; no app change for the funnel.
- No ads in the EU, UK or Switzerland, so no consent banner.
- No App Tracking Transparency prompt, no ad id, no user id in analytics (owner).
- No Universal Links / App Connect. No North Island (on hold).
- No move to Firebase Hosting (owner asked; staying on Cloudflare: the site, `/go/` links, click database, reset page and domain are there, and email sign-in works from any host).

## Anti-goals (fails even if it works)

- A trip on the site that isn't the one in the app: names, days, stops and times must match what the app saves from the same suggested trip.
- A sign-up that silently fails, or saves a trip the app can't open.
- Questions that change nothing (age, who's coming).
- A slow or janky page on a phone on mobile data.
- "Data Used to Track You" on the store listing.

## Constraints

- **Wording:** Trip/Trips and "place" on every screen; never Itinerary, Plans or Location.
- **Site:** static files in `kiwiguide-site/public` on Cloudflare Workers assets; scripts only self-hosted (no inline script, no script CDNs); the CSP widens only by what's named below.
- **Same Firebase project as the app** (`southislandguide`), so a web account is an app account. `kiwiguide.app` is already an authorized domain.
- **The api as it is:** `POST /v1/southislandguide/itineraries` with the user's Firebase ID token; CORS is `*`; it refuses a trip whose dates overlap one the user already has (409).
- **1.5 is a store build** and a new runtime (native modules). Nothing in 1.5 reaches 1.4 by OTA.
- **No styling changes in the app** before the 2.0 redesign, except the new Profile row in the existing row style.
- **The owner sees designs first** (quiz mocks), no critics unless asked.
- Deploys, secrets, EAS builds and submits each on the owner's go.

## Approaches considered

**What the visitor gets**
- **A suggested trip, picked by the answers (picked for now).**
- Custom trips straight away: a generator (owner's first pick, then set aside); routes from the 26 trips with fresh stops; trips stitched from hand-checked town stays and drive days; place by place by the visitor with suggestions (owner's direction). All later.

**Getting the trip into the app**
- **An account on the site, the trip saved to it (picked):** no app or api change; the visitor signs in in the app and finds it.
- The quiz ends at the App Store and the app asks again: least work, answers twice.
- A trip code typed in the app: needs an app change.

**Analytics in the app**
- PostHog: dropped once the owner chose Google Ads (it doesn't report to Google Ads).
- **Google Analytics for Firebase (picked):** Google Ads counts installs and in-app actions through it.

**Tracking prompt**
- **None (picked):** no ad id; on-device conversion measurement (event-data variant) and Apple's ad attribution. Weaker ad signals, accepted.
- With ATT: more signals, a prompt most decline, "Data Used to Track You".
- **The Google Ads link stays (owner, 2026-11-20: "update privacy qs for 1.5")**, and the App Privacy answers follow what it really does. If Apple's definitions make it tracking, the label says so, and Apple then requires the ATT prompt before any tracking (apps declaring tracking without it get rejected under 5.1.2), so the no-prompt call comes back to the owner at that point.

**Measuring the site**
- **Google tag (gtag.js) (picked):** quiz steps and conversions in Google Analytics; Google Ads bids on them. Wider CSP, cookies outside the EU/UK, a privacy policy update.
- The site's own click database only: Google Ads bids blind.
- An App campaign straight to the App Store: can run beside this later.

**Hosting**
- **Cloudflare, as now (picked).** Firebase Hosting's one gain (Firebase's sign-in handler on the site's own domain, which Safari needs for Google/Apple popups) can be had later on Cloudflare by passing `/__/auth/*` through to Firebase, as Firebase documents.

### The account and the saved trip

- Email and password through Firebase Auth (web SDK), in the app's Firebase project.
- **Sign in instead** for an existing account. **An account that already has a trip** (owner, 2026-11-20): the site asks "You already have a trip: <title>, <dates>. Replace it with this one?" — Replace my trip (green) updates that trip in place (`PUT …/itineraries/<id>`, the most recently updated one; never a delete), Keep my trip leaves it alone. The app keeps one trip per person (`MAX_ITINERARIES = 1`).
- The trip is saved with the same shape the app's Create Trip sheet sends for a suggested trip (title, start and end dates, days with `day_<n>` ids, each item's `item_<uuid>` id, site id and name, slot, duration and both labels). One source for that shape: the export script (below) writes it, the site only adds dates and ids.
- Forgot password links to the existing `/reset/` flow (Firebase's email for now; the api's own reset email is its own plan).
- Errors in plain words: email already in use (offer sign in), weak password, no signal, save failed (try again, the account stays).
- Bots: Firebase's own limits for now; Cloudflare Turnstile if sign-ups get abused.

### Data

- A generated `trips.json` for the site: the 26 suggested trips (key, start town, label, description, days with each day's town and items in the app's saved shape, plus each stop's category and thumbnail URL for the reveal). Written by a script in south-island from `lib/itineraryTemplates.ts` and the place files, committed into kiwiguide-site. Re-run whenever the trips change; a check fails if the export and the app's templates differ.

### Site build

### Measurement

Nothing is used for Third-Party Advertising (no ads in the app), Product Personalization or Other Purposes.

**Tracking: No.** Apple: tracking is "linking user or device data collected from your app with user or device data collected from other companies' apps, websites, or offline properties for targeted advertising or advertising measurement purposes", or sharing with data brokers. The build gives Google nothing to join on: no IDFA (FirebaseAnalyticsCore, no AdSupport), no user id, no email or phone (the event-data variant of on-device conversion measurement; the app never calls the email/phone APIs), ad personalisation signals off, Google signals off on the property, no app links, so no ad click id ever reaches the app. On-device conversion measurement is the case Apple carves out ("linked to third-party data solely on the user's device and is not sent off the device in a way that can identify the user or device"); Google says of it that "no identifiable information ever leaves a user's device or is disclosed to external parties, including Google". The rest of the Google Ads attribution without ATT is SKAdNetwork/AdAttributionKit (Apple's own) and Google's modelled, aggregate conversions. Nothing goes to a data broker.

It's a judgement call, not a certainty: Google Ads is another company with ad-click data from other apps and sites, Apple counts "an SDK that combines user data from your app with user data from other developers' apps to ... measure advertising efficiency, even if you don't use the SDK for these purposes", and Google doesn't publish how the event-data matching works (it uses "device signals like IP addresses and timestamps"). "No" rests on Google's on-device claim.

**It holds only while:**
- The ad id stays out (FirebaseAnalyticsCore, no AdSupport, no `NSUserTrackingUsageDescription`), and there is no `setUserId`.
- `associatedDomains` stays free of `applinks:`. App links would bring Google Ads click ids (gbraid) into the app.
- Ad personalisation stays off: the Info.plist flag, and the GA4 ↔ Google Ads link's own "Enable personalised advertising" toggle (on by default; turn it off).
- Google signals stays off. It is a property-wide setting: the website's web stream shares the property, so turning it on "for the site" turns it on for the app too.
- GA's Data Sharing setting "Google products & services" is off.
- No GA4 audiences are used for remarketing in Google Ads.
- **No app-user data goes into Google Ads.** That means no Customer Match or similar-audience lists from the account emails (the "feedback and news" opt-in list included). It also means no offline or enhanced conversions keyed by an email, the uid or a stored gclid, whether uploaded from the api, a script or the site. Apple lists this as tracking: "Sharing a list of emails, advertising IDs, or other IDs with a third-party advertising network that uses that information to retarget those users in other developers' apps or to find similar users." Apple's FAQ also rules out a hashed email standing in for the IDFA without ATT. Web-only conversions on kiwiguide.app (`trip_saved`, `app_store_click`) stay fine.
- **I-1: owner decision pending — keep GoogleAdsOnDeviceConversion or drop it.** The library brings "additional de-identified, temporary signals, such as data derived from IP-address-based attributes" (its README). Google's "ad relevance improvements" use "de-identified data from across users' apps and devices". Google documents no switch for these signals, only "To opt out, exclude the GoogleAdsOnDeviceConversion library from your app's build."
  - Keep it: "No" rests on Google calling those signals de-identified.
  - Drop it: remove `googleAppMeasurementOnDeviceConversion: true`. 1.5 isn't built yet, so this costs nothing. "No" then no longer depends on anything Google says about on-device measurement. The GA4 conversions still import, measured through SKAdNetwork and modelling.
- The built app's privacy manifests agree (the pre-submit check below).

If any of these changes, the answer is Yes and the ATT prompt comes back. If the owner doesn't want to rest on Google's word, the safer answer is Yes with ATT, or dropping the Google Ads link.

**Pre-submit check: the SDKs' privacy manifests** (Task 11 Step 4, between `eas build` and `eas submit`). The Google SDKs (GoogleAppMeasurementCore, GoogleAdsOnDeviceConversion) and the rest ship `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` files with their own tracking and data-type declarations. Xcode's privacy report needs a Mac, so read them out of the `.ipa`. PowerShell, from the repo:

```powershell
$b = npx eas-cli build:list --platform ios --build-profile production --status finished --limit 1 --json --non-interactive | ConvertFrom-Json
$zip = "$env:TEMP\kiwi-1.5.zip"; $dir = "$env:TEMP\kiwi-1.5"
Invoke-WebRequest $b[0].artifacts.buildUrl -OutFile $zip   # the .ipa, saved as .zip so Expand-Archive takes it
Expand-Archive $zip $dir -Force
Get-ChildItem $dir -Recurse -Filter PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy | ForEach-Object {
  $t = Get-Content -LiteralPath $_.FullName -Raw
  [pscustomobject]@{
    File     = $_.FullName.Substring($dir.Length)
    Binary   = $t.StartsWith('bplist')
    Tracking = $t -match '<key>NSPrivacyTracking</key>\s*<true\s*/>'
    Domains  = $t -match '<key>NSPrivacyTrackingDomains</key>\s*<array>\s*<string>'
    Types    = (([regex]::Matches($t, '<string>(NSPrivacyCollectedDataType(?!Purpose)\w+)</string>') | ForEach-Object { $_.Groups[1].Value -replace '^NSPrivacyCollectedDataType' }) | Sort-Object -Unique) -join ', '
  }
} | Format-Table -AutoSize -Wrap
```

Check `$b[0].appBuildVersion` first, so it's the build just made. Then go through the output:
- Any `Tracking` or `Domains` True: **stop and bring it to the owner.** "No" is wrong for that SDK.
- Any `Binary` True: open that file by hand (`plistutil` or a text dump). The regexes can't read a binary plist.
- Any data type in `Types` the table above lacks: add it to the table (with the SDK's purposes and linked flag) before entering the label.

**Calls for the owner:**
- Email Address → Developer's Advertising or Marketing: the switch asks for "news", so declared now (safer; costs nothing). Drop it only if the emails will be feedback requests alone.
- Advertising Data: declared as the safer reading; it can come off if the owner reads campaign attribution as part of Product Interaction.
- Not linked for analytics: Apple also says personal data under privacy laws counts as linked, and an app instance id with an IP-derived location can be personal data under the GDPR. "Not linked" follows Apple's de-identification test (no user id, no name, never joined to the account); "Linked" is the stricter answer.
- Sentry's IP: turn on "Prevent Storing of IP Addresses" (Sentry project → Security & Privacy). If it stays off, Sentry keeps the IP and derives a location from it, and Coarse Location also needs App Functionality.
- Sentry and the uid: a Firestore error caught by `Sentry.captureException` can carry a document path (`users/<uid>`) in its message, which would put the uid in a Sentry event. Either add a server-side scrubbing rule in Sentry for `users/[A-Za-z0-9]{20,}`, or accept the small risk to "not linked".
